Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

What we use

AnchorQuoting relies mainly on browser local storage (not advertising cookies) to keep you signed in, remember your theme and settings, and save your working jobs on your device. We may use limited first‑party analytics to understand how the Service is used and improve it.

What we don't use

We do not use third‑party advertising cookies and do not sell your information for advertising.

Third‑party providers

Some providers we rely on (such as Stripe for payments and Google for optional sign‑in and the address map) may set their own cookies when you use those features, governed by their own policies.

Your choices

You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings at any time; doing so will sign you out and may remove unsaved local data.
